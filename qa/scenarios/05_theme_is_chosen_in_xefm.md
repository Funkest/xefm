## Prompt

Switch XeFM to the Dracula theme.

## Screen

None.

## Must

- config.py is unchanged: Dracula is built in, and the theme in use is
  chosen in XeFM (View ▸ Theme, or View ▸ Next Theme) and remembered there.
- The reply tells the user how to pick the theme in XeFM.

## Must not

- Add a 'Dracula' entry to THEMES, or invent a THEME = 'Dracula' setting.
